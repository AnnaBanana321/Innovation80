A Pearson’s chi-square test with Yates’ continuity correction was conducted to examine the relationship between survey time point (Pre vs. Post) and the perception that the younger person was uninterested in getting to know someone older. The results indicated a statistically significant association, X²(1, N = [sample size]) = 6.87, p = .009.

The significant result suggests that perceptions of younger individuals’ disinterest in engaging with older individuals changed between the Pre and Post survey conditions. Specifically, participants were significantly less likely to perceive younger individuals as uninterested in getting to know older people in the Post survey compared to the Pre survey. This finding may indicate a positive shift in intergenerational engagement following the intervention. However, further research is needed to determine the extent and causes of this shift.
